# Bidding & Financial Strategy (PTA Ref: P52985)

## 1. Tender Overview & Selection Method

- **Procurement Type**: Non-Consultancy Services
- **Bidding Procedure**: Single Stage - One Envelope
- **Selection Technique**: **Least Cost Based Selection (LCBS)**
  > *Note on LCBS*: In LCBS, all bids that meet the mandatory technical eligibility criteria are evaluated purely on price. The lowest priced technically compliant bidder wins the contract. Therefore, the financial strategy must be competitive while ensuring all technical ToRs are 100% compliant.

---

## 2. Mandatory Eligibility Criteria Checklist

To qualify for evaluation, the bidder must submit the following mandatory documents on EPADS v2.0:

| # | Eligibility Requirement | Status / Document Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Legal Registration** | Certificate of Incorporation / Form C / Partnership Deed / CNIC |
| **2** | **Tax Compliance** | Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Certificate from FBR (NTN & STRN/GSTN) |
| **3** | **Relevant Experience** | Proof of relevant experience (Purchase Orders / Work Orders / Contracts for hosting WhatsApp solutions or similar digital platforms) |
| **4** | **Registration Authority** | Registration with relevant government/telecom/IT authority in Pakistan |
| **5** | **Bid Securing Instrument** | Form 9: Bid Securing Declaration (scanned copy on EPADS v2.0 + original submitted to PTA F-5/1 Islamabad prior to deadline) |
| **6** | **Disqualification Declaration** | Affidavit/Undertaking that the bidder is not blacklisted by PPRA, PTA, or any government entity |
