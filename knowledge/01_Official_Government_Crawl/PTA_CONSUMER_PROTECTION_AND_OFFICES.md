# PTA Consumer Protection, Complaints Redressal & Zonal Offices

## Consumer Support & Helpline
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: `0800-55055` (Available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
- **Online Complaint Management System (CMS)**: https://complaint.pta.gov.pk/
- **PTA CMS Mobile App**: Available on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.
- **Complaint Escalation**: If service provider (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, Ufone, PTCL, StormFiber, Nayatel) fails to resolve complaint within statutory timeframe (7 days for billing, 48 hours for service outage), escalate to PTA CMS.

## Reporting Illegal Content & Cyber Concerns
- **Blasphemous / Obscene Content**: Report to `complaint@pta.gov.pk` or PTA CMS.
- **Stolen Mobile Blocking**: Call `0800-55055` or email `imc@pta.gov.pk` with device IMEI, police complaint/FIR, and CNIC.
- **Unsolicited / Promotional SMS Blocking**: Register on "Do Not Call Register" (DNCR) by texting `reg` to `3627` or contact operator.
