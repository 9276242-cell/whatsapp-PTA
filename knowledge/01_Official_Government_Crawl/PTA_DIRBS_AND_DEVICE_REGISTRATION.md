# PTA DIRBS & Mobile Device Registration (Official Regulatory Standard)

## Overview & Legal Mandate
Under the Device Identification, Registration & Blocking System (DIRBS) Regulations established by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA), all mobile devices utilizing a SIM card within Pakistan must be verified, type-approved, and registered. Unregistered devices are automatically blocked from accessing any cellular network (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone/PTCL) after 60 days.

## 4 Methods to Register Mobile Devices
1. **Online Web Portal**: Visit the official DIRBS portal at https://dirbs.pta.gov.pk/drs
2. **USSD Code (Local SIM)**: Dial `*8484#` from any mobile phone connected to a Pakistani network.
3. **Franchise / Customer Service Centers**: Visit any mobile operator's authorized service center (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, Ufone) with original CNIC and device.
4. **Official Video Guide**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4MbpMlwv0yI

## Critical Rules & Statutory Limits
- **60-Day Grace Window**: Registration and customs duty payment must be completed within 60 days of inserting a local Pakistani SIM.
- **Quota Limit**: Maximum of 5 mobile devices allowed per CNIC per calendar year.
- **Historical Cutoff**: All mobile devices active and connected to Pakistani networks on or before 15 January 2019 are automatically grandfathered/registered. Devices used after 15 January 2019 require mandatory registration.
- **Warranty Replacements**: For defective devices replaced under manufacturer warranty, users must submit an official claim through DIRBS with replacement verification from the authorized OEM dealer.
- **Checking Device Status (IMEI)**:
  - Dial `*#06#` to get the 15-digit IMEI.
  - Send the 15-digit IMEI via SMS to `8484` or check online at https://dirbs.pta.gov.pk
  - Statuses: Compliant (PTA Approved), Valid (GSMA valid, needs duty payment), Non-Compliant (Counterfeit/Cloned, subject to block).

## FBR Customs Duty & Tax Payment
- Once registered via DIRBS, the system generates an official 17-digit Payment Slip ID (PSID).
- PSID can be paid via: 1Link 1Bill (Online Banking Apps, Easypaisa, JazzCash, ATMs, or designated bank branches).
- Commercial vs Personal:
  - Personal: Travelers & Overseas Pakistanis (Passport-based registration within 60 days grants concessional rates).
  - Commercial: Importers must possess Type Approval and COC (Certificate of Compliance).
