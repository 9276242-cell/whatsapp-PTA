13 FAQsClick a question to expand
Q1What is LSDS?+
LSDS (Lost & Stolen Device System) is a PTA service that helps you block or unblock your mobile phone if it is lost, stolen, or snatched.
Q2How can I block my lost or stolen mobile phone?+
The complainant shall use CMS portal i.e. the PTA CMS Mobile App (Android/iOS) or visit https://complaint.pta.gov.pk/registeruser.aspx
Q3What details are required to submit a complaint on CMS?+
Complainant credentials:
Mandatory: Name, Father name, CNIC number, City
Optional: Email address, postal address
Mobile number used within last 6 months in the stolen, snatched or missing mobile device
SIM used in the device must be registered to complainant’s CNIC
IMEI number(s) of the device
Device details: brand, model, colour
Reason for blocking
Incident location/Area and Incident City and incident date
FIR number if the incident is reported to authorities (optional)
If there is no operational SIM in the second slot till incident date, then the complainant shall provide only one IMEI along with the MSISDN being used
A unique reference number shall be sent via SMS required for tracking of complaint and future unblocking.
Q4How will I receive OTP during complaint submission?+
OTP shall be sent to the SIM used in the lost/stolen handset (must be registered to complainant’s CNIC).
Q5Can I block my phone if no SIM was used in it?+
No, the device must have been used with a Pakistani SIM to process the request through CMS.
Q6What happens if my phone has two IMEIs, but I have used only one SIM?+
If your device has two IMEIs, blocking one IMEI will automatically block the other (for PTA-approved devices).
Q7What if my IMEI is not recognized or shows mismatch?+
In your case, provided IMEI and mobile number are not matched or provided mobile number is not used against the mentioned IMEI, therefore, please provide valid information and register online complaint accordingly.
Q8What should I do if I cannot submit the complaint online?+
You can:
Call PTA helpline 0800-55055, or
Visit the nearest PTA office for assistance
Q9Can I block a phone that was lost long ago?+
Yes, you can still submit a complaint if you have the correct IMEI and required details.
Q10How can I check my IMEI status?+
You can check by:
Sending IMEI to 8484 via SMS
Using the DIRBS website
Using the DVS mobile app
Q11What happens after my phone is blocked?+
The phone will not work on any mobile network in Pakistan.
Q12Can PTA help recover my stolen phone?+
No, for recovery you should contact:
Local Police, or
CPLC (in relevant cities)
Q13How can I unblock the stolen device that has been recovered?+
Unblocking provision shall be bound to only person’s credentials which had been produced during blocking request
(i) Submit unblocking request through CMS
(ii) Provide previous device blocking complaint reference number and CNIC number for confirmation
(iii) OTP shall be sent on mobile number provided for blocking request
(iv) CMS shall verify and process the unblocking of device
(v) Confirmation shall be sent through SMS/email.
(vi) IMEI number(s) shall be unblocked within 24 hours
