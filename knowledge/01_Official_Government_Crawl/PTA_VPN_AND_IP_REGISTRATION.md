# PTA IP & VPN Registration for IT Exporters, Call Centers, & Freelancers

## Regulatory Framework
Under Section 31(1) of the Pakistan Telecommunication (Re-organization) Act 1996 and Telecom Regulations, all encrypted communications and Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) operating over commercial internet lines must be registered to prevent grey traffic and ensure cyber security.

## Two Compliance Routes
1. **PTA-Licensed VPN Providers**:
   - Entities purchasing VPN connectivity from PTA CVAS-Data licensed providers do NOT require separate registration.
2. **Direct IP & VPN Portal Registration (Free of Cost)**:
   - Official Portal: https://ipregistration.pta.gov.pk/
   - Turnaround Time: Approvals are usually processed within 8 to 24 business hours (1 working day).

## Eligibility & Requirements
- **Software Houses / IT Exporters**:
  - SECP Incorporation / Form-A / Form-29 / NTN.
  - PSEB (Pakistan Software Export Board) Membership Certificate.
  - Static Public IP address provided by authorized ISP.
- **Call Centers**:
  - Valid PTA Call Center Registration Certificate.
  - Static IP and list of destination server IPs/domains.
- **Freelancers**:
  - Letter of employment / client contract / platform verification (Upwork, Fiverr, TopTal profile link & invoice).
  - Undertaking on stamp paper or declared terms.
  - Static IP from home broadband ISP.
