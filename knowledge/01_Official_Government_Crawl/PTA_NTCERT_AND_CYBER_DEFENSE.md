# National Telecom Computer Emergency Response Team (nTCERT) & Cyber Defense Framework

## 1. Overview of nTCERT
The **National Telecom Computer Emergency Response Team (nTCERT)** is established under the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) to serve as the premier cybersecurity coordination center and emergency readiness unit for Pakistan's telecommunication sector.
- **Official Portal**: `https://ntcert.pta.gov.pk`
- **Primary Mandate**: Safeguarding Critical Telecom Data and Infrastructure (CTDI), coordinating incident response across Cellular Mobile Operators (CMOs) and Internet Service Providers (ISPs), issuing threat advisories, and building national cybersecurity resilience.

---

## 2. National & Strategic Collaborations (`https://ntcert.pta.gov.pk/national.html`)
PTA and nTCERT lead cross-agency collaborations to protect Pakistan's digital ecosystem:

### A. The National Cyber Emergency Response Team of Pakistan (National CERT)
- Formal high-level partnership established between National CERT and PTA Headquarters, Islamabad.
- Dedicated operational and technical interface established for real-time threat intelligence sharing and incident mitigation.
- Collaborative defense of Pakistan’s core backbone networks, submarine cable landing stations, and national IXPs (Internet Exchange Points).

### B. Securities & Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) Partnership
- **MOU Signed**: Strategic alliance between PTA and SECP to secure digital financial technologies and protect citizens from cyber financial fraud.
- **Predatory Lending App Crackdown**: Established a joint operational mechanism to identify, monitor, and remove unauthorized/illegal digital nano-lending apps from app stores and block their servers.
- **Digital Financial Sector Cybersecurity**: Formulation and enforcement of cybersecurity frameworks for FinTech and Non-Banking Financial Institutions (NBFIs).
- **Public Awareness Campaigns**: Joint nationwide consumer education initiatives advising against untrusted loan apps and unauthorized credential sharing.

### C. International CERT Alliances (`https://ntcert.pta.gov.pk/international.html`)
- Active member of global cybersecurity federations including the **Asia Pacific Computer Emergency Response Team (APCERT)** and the **OIC-CERT**.
- Cross-border threat hunting, zero-day vulnerability intelligence exchange, and coordinated vulnerability disclosure.

## 3. Core Regulations, Policies & Strategies
All telecom licensees (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, Ufone, PTCL, Nayatel, Wateen, Cybernet, etc.) must strictly adhere to the following PTA cybersecurity regulations:

1. **CTDISR 2020 & Framework 2022 (Critical Telecom Data and Infrastructure Security Regulations)**:
   - Mandatory security benchmarks, data classification, encryption, and business continuity for all Tier-1 and Tier-2 telecom infrastructure.
2. **Telecom Sector Cyber Security Strategy (2023–2028)**:
   - 5-year strategic blueprint establishing proactive threat defense, sovereign CERT capabilities, automated SOC (Security Operations Center) telemetry, and human capital development.
3. **5G Security Guidelines (2026)**:
   - Security specifications for Open RAN, network slicing isolation, edge computing virtualization, and supply-chain risk auditing for 5G commercial rollouts.
4. **Legal Anchors**:
   - Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA), 2016
   - National Cyber Security Policy, 2021
   - Establishment of Telecommunication Appellate Tribunal Act, 2024

---

## 4. Cyber Security Third-Party Audit Firms (`https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/security-audit-firms-1041671834-2023-05-30`)
- Telecom operators are legally required to undergo regular independent cybersecurity and compliance audits by PTA-registered third-party audit firms.
- PTA maintains strict registration criteria, categorisation of licensees, and certified firm rosters ensuring compliance with CTDISR standards.

---

## 5. Advisories & Capacity Building
- **Advisories Hub (`https://ntcert.pta.gov.pk/2025/Advisories/advisories.html`)**: Rapid alerts and mitigation directives on critical zero-days, Apache, Windows, Cisco, GitLab, WordPress vulnerabilities, and ransomware outbreak signatures.
- **Incident Reporting & Guidance**:
  - Immediate incident notification protocols for licensed telecom operators.
  - Phishing defenses, ransomware defense checklists, and safe biometric verification tips for consumers.
- **Trainings & Exercises**: Annual national cyber drill workshops, Incident Response & Forensics bootcamps, and IPv6 secure transition workshops for CMOs and ISPs.
