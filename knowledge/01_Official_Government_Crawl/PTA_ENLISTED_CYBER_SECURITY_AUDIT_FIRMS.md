# Official Register of Enlisted Third-Party Cybersecurity Audit Firms (PTA)

## 1. Statutory Mandate & Audit Hierarchy Rules
Under the **Critical Telecom Data and Infrastructure Security Regulations (CTDISR)** and the **Cyber Security Strategy for Telecom Sector (2023–2028)**, all PTA licensees (Cellular Mobile Operators, LDI, Local Loop, ISPs, Data Centers) are legally required to undergo regular independent third-party cybersecurity audits by PTA-registered audit firms.
- **Official Source**: `https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/security-audit-firms-categorization-1547609365-2023-05-30`
- **Hierarchy Rule**: Audit firms can perform audits of licensees in their approved category or downward in the hierarchy:
  - **Category I**: Authorized to audit all telecom licensees across **Cat-I, Cat-II, Cat-III, and Cat-IV**.
  - **Category II**: Authorized to audit licensees falling under **Cat-II, Cat-III, and Cat-IV**.
  - **Category III**: Authorized to audit licensees falling under **Cat-III and Cat-IV**.
  - **Category IV**: Authorized to audit **Cat-IV** licensees only (cannot audit upwards).
