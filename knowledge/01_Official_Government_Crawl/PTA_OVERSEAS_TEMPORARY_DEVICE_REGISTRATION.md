# PTA Temporary Device Registration for Overseas Pakistanis & Foreign Nationals

## 1. Facility Overview
The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA), in coordination with Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) and Federal Investigation Agency (FIA), provides a **Free Temporary Mobile Device Registration Facility** for Overseas Pakistanis and Foreign Nationals visiting Pakistan on short-term visits.
- **Allowed Validity**: Up to **120 days** of uninterrupted cellular connectivity per visit.
- **Cost / Duty**: **100% Free** — No customs duty or commercial taxes apply during the 120-day validity window.
- **Eligibility**:
  - Overseas Pakistanis holding a valid Pakistani passport / NICOP.
  - Foreign nationals holding a foreign passport with a valid Pakistani visa or visa-on-arrival entry stamp.
  - Can be availed on **every single visit** to Pakistan!

---

## 2. Mandatory Eligibility & Verification Rules
1. **FIA Verification**: Applicant's entry into Pakistan is automatically verified in real-time through the FIA Integrated Border Management System (IBMS). Applications submitted prior to crossing Pakistani immigration will not be validated.
2. **Local Network IP Requirement**:
   - The DIRBS registration portal (`https://dirbs.pta.gov.pk/drs`) is **accessible only from within the territorial boundaries and IP networks of Pakistan**.
   - It cannot be accessed while abroad on foreign IP networks.
3. **SIM Pairing**:
   - The device's IMEI is paired specifically with the local Pakistani mobile SIM number(s) registered under the applicant’s CNIC/Passport.
   - Only the paired SIM card(s) will function in the registered IMEI slots during the 120-day period.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Online Registration Procedure
1. **Account Creation**:
   - Connect to local Wi-Fi or mobile data upon arrival in Pakistan.
   - Visit: `https://dirbs.pta.gov.pk/drs` (or direct path `https://dirbs.pta.gov.pk/drs/auth/foreigners_temporary_unblocking`).
   - Fill in your details (Full Name, Email, Passport Number, Nationality).
2. **Account Activation**:
   - Click the activation link sent to your email address to activate your DIRBS profile.
3. **Application Submission**:
   - Login to DIRBS and select **"Temporary Registration for Overseas Pakistanis / Foreign Nationals"**.
   - Enter your **Passport Number**, **Date of Entry into Pakistan**, and **Tentative Departure Date**.
   - Enter device **IMEI numbers** (IMEI 1 and IMEI 2 for dual SIM phones). Check IMEIs by dialing `*#06#`.
   - Provide the **local Pakistani SIM mobile number(s)** that will be used inside the phone.
4. **OTP Verification**:
   - One-Time Passwords (OTPs) are dispatched to both your registered email address and your Pakistani mobile number.
   - Enter the OTPs to finalize the application.
5. **Immediate Activation**:
   - Device pairing and unblocking occur automatically after verification against FIA immigration records.

---

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- **Q: What happens after 120 days?**
  - If the device remains in Pakistan beyond 120 days, it must be permanently registered by paying applicable FBR customs duties and taxes through DIRBS; otherwise, cellular functionality on Pakistani networks will be suspended.
- **Q: Can I use this on my next trip?**
  - **Yes!** Overseas Pakistanis and tourists can avail this free 120-day facility on each subsequent entry into Pakistan.
- **Q: How many devices can be temporarily registered?**
  - Up to two devices per individual traveler per visit.
- **Q: Where do I get help if my device doesn't activate?**
  - PTA Toll-Free Consumer Helpline: **0800-55055** (09:00 AM – 05:00 PM, Mon–Fri).
  - PTA Online CMS: `https://complaint.pta.gov.pk/userlogin.aspx`
  - Video Tutorial: Official PTA YouTube Guide (`https://youtu.be/Z3zeeD7468A`).
