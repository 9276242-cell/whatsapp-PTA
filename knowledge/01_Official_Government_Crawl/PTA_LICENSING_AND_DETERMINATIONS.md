# PTA Licensing Framework, Authorizations & Determinations

## Key Telecom License Categories
1. **Long Distance International (LDI)**:
   - Grants rights to establish and operate telecom transmission facilities connecting national gateway exchanges with international switching centers.
   - Application Form & Criteria: Available under PTA Telecom Regulation Portal (https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/act-1397047816-2023-05-30). Requires corporate SECP registration, technical rollout plan, financial net worth evaluation, and security clearances.
2. **Local Loop (LL)**:
   - Fixed-line local telecom network operations per Telecom Region.
3. **Class Value Added Services (CVAS)**:
   - **CVAS-Data**: Internet Service Providers (ISPs), Data Services, VPN Service Providers.
   - **CVAS-Voice**: Audio conferencing, Call Center connectivity.
4. **IP / VPN Whitelisting**:
   - Software exporters, call centers, and freelancers register static IPs and VPN usage for unthrottled, encrypted traffic via https://ipregistration.pta.gov.pk/.

## Authority's Determinations & Regulatory Orders
- Official repository: https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/authoritys-determinations-1069818154-2023-05-30
- Covers spectrum fee determinations, tariff approvals, inter-operator interconnection disputes, and consumer quality of service (QoS) enforcement orders.
