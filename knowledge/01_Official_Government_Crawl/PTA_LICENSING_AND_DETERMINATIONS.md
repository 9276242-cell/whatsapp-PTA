# PTA Statutory Functions, Licensing Framework & Authority Determinations

## 1. Statutory Functions of PTA
Under Section 4 of the **Pakistan Telecommunication (Re-organization) Act, 1996 (as amended)**:
1. **Regulate Telecommunication Systems**: Oversee the establishment, operation, and maintenance of all telecommunication networks and provision of services across Pakistan, AJK, and Gilgit-Baltistan.
2. **Promote Fair Competition & Consumer Rights**: Prevent monopolistic behavior, protect consumer rights, ensure quality of service (QoS) standards, and maintain affordable tariffs.
3. **Radio-Frequency Spectrum Allocation**: Receive and expeditiously process applications for radio-frequency spectrum in coordination with the **Frequency Allocation Board (FAB)**.
4. **Licensing & Enforcement**: Issue, modify, or revoke licenses; ensure compliance with license conditions and national security standards.
5. **Judicial & Appellate Recourse**: Decisions are subject to appeal before the **Telecommunication Appellate Tribunal** established under the **Telecommunication Appellate Tribunal Act, 2024**.

## 2. Key Telecom Licensing Categories
1. **Cellular Mobile Operators (CMO)**: Nationwide 2G/3G/4G/5G mobile network operation licenses (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, Ufone, SCO).
2. **Long Distance International (LDI)**: Operates international gateways, cable landing stations, and long-haul inter-city fiber backbones.
3. **Local Loop (LL) & Wireless Local Loop (WLL)**: Fixed-line/wireless last-mile connectivity within designated telecom regions.
4. **Class Value Added Services (CVAS)**:
   - **CVAS-Data**: Internet Service Providers (ISPs), public Wi-Fi, data transmission.
   - **CVAS-Voice**: Audio conferencing, corporate call centers, premium rate numbers.
5. **Infrastructure Provider (IP) Licenses**: Tower sharing, dark fiber leasing, telecom civil infrastructure.

## 3. Notable Authority Determinations (`https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/authoritys-determinations-1069818154-2023-05-30`)
- **PTML Digital Product — "ONIC"**: Authority determination regulating the launch of PTML (Ufone/Pak Telecom Mobile Limited) app-based digital telecom brand "ONIC", with dedicated prepaid SIMs, online biometric doorstep delivery, and bundle packaging.
- **Prepaid Balance Validity Period Determination**: Prevents mobile operators from prematurely confiscating customer account balance; mandates transparent validity timelines tied to recharge amounts.
- **eSIM Issuance Charges Determination**: Regulates consumer pricing ceilings for physical-to-eSIM conversions and new eSIM activations across all operators.
- **Mobile Number Portability (MNP) Regulations**: Allows consumers to switch telecom operators while retaining their existing 11-digit mobile number within 24–48 hours without artificial friction.
- **Interconnection & Tariff Approvals**: Prevents predatory pricing and mandates fair interconnection rates among operators.
