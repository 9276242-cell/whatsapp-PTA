# PTA SIM Information & Biometric Verification System (668 Service)

## Checking Registered SIMs
- **SMS Method**: Send your 13-digit CNIC (without dashes or spaces) to `668`.
- **Online Portal**: Visit https://cnic.sims.pk to check registered SIM count across all operators.

## Regulatory SIM Limits & Policies
- **Maximum Allowance**: Up to 8 SIMs allowed per CNIC:
  - Maximum 5 Voice/Calling SIMs.
  - Maximum 3 Data/Broadband Only SIMs.
- **Activation Timeframe**: New SIMs are activated within 24 hours of successful Biometric Verification System (BVS) at an authorized telecom outlet.
- **Disowning Unwanted/Fraudulent SIMs**:
  - A SIM can be officially disowned after 60 days of issuance.
  - Requires physical visit to the respective operator's Customer Care Center with original CNIC for biometric disownment.
- **Foreign Nationals**: Must present an original valid Passport with valid Pakistani Visa. SIM validity is tied directly to visa duration.
- **Deceased Persons' SIMs**: Can be blocked or transferred by legal heirs upon presenting original Death Certificate and NADRA Family Registration Certificate (FRC).
- **Minors (Under 18)**: Eligible for SIM issuance if biometrics/Juvenile Card records exist with NADRA, linked to guardian CNIC.
