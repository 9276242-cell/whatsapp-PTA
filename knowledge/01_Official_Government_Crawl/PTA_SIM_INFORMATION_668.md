# PTA SIM Information, 668 Service & Biometric Verification System (MBVS)

## 1. Checking Registered SIMs
- **SMS Method**: Send your 13-digit CNIC (without dashes or spaces) to `668`.
- **Online Verification Portal**: Visit `https://cnic.sims.pk` to view live registered SIM counts categorized by Cellular Mobile Operator (Jazz, Telenor, Zong, Ufone, SCO).

---

## 2. Multi Random Finger Biometric Verification System (MBVS)
PTA, in collaboration with Cellular Mobile Operators (CMOs) and NADRA, has mandated the **Multi Random Finger Biometric Verification System (MBVS)**.
- **Mandatory Transactions Requiring MBVS**:
  1. Buying & Activating a new SIM
  2. Issuance of a Duplicate SIM / Replacement SIM
  3. Change of Ownership of a SIM
  4. Mobile Number Portability (MNP)
  5. Biometric Re-verification of existing numbers
  6. Disowning unauthorized / unknown SIMs
- **How MBVS Works**:
  - The system dynamically prompts for random finger/thumb combinations instead of static single thumbprints, eliminating biometric replay attacks, silicone spoofing, and identity theft.
  - A maximum of two successful finger impressions verified online with NADRA database are required.

---

## 3. Strict Consumer Safety & Anti-Fraud Warnings
- **Never Give Fingerprints on Paper**: Fraudsters masquerade as government support scheme agents to collect fingerprints on paper or standalone dummy scanners. Never put thumbprints on plain paper or unverified devices!
- **Only Visit Authorized Outlets**: Only carry out SIM transactions at authorized Customer Service Centers (CSC), Franchisees, or registered retailers displaying valid MBVS certification.
- **Ensure SIM Handover**: Always collect your newly issued SIM immediately after biometric verification. If an agent does not hand over the SIM, lodge an immediate complaint with PTA.

---

## 4. Regulatory SIM Limits & Ownership Rules
- **Maximum Allowance**: Up to 8 SIMs per CNIC:
  - Maximum 5 Voice/Calling SIMs.
  - Maximum 3 Data/Broadband Only SIMs.
- **Disowning Unwanted / Fraudulent SIMs**:
  - Can be officially disowned at operator Customer Service Centers with original CNIC.
- **Foreign Nationals**:
  - Valid Passport with valid Pakistani Visa required. SIM validity is synchronized with the visa expiration date.
- **Deceased Persons' SIMs**:
  - Can be transferred or cancelled by legal heirs upon presenting the original Death Certificate and NADRA Family Registration Certificate (FRC).
- **Minors (Under 18)**:
  - Permitted if biometric/Juvenile Card records exist with NADRA, linked to a parent/guardian CNIC.
