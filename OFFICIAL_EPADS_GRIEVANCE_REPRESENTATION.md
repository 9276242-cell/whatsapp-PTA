## SECTION 1: LEGAL AND PROCEDURAL GROUNDS FOR GRIEVANCE

### 1. Violation of Rule 10 (Clear and Non-Restrictive Specifications)
Under Rule 10 of PPR 2004, procuring agencies are mandated to lay down specifications that provide an equal and clear basis for competitive bidding. The subject SBD lacks critical technical data required to size infrastructure, including:
- Expected daily/monthly active message volumes.
- Peak concurrent conversation throughput (Queries Per Second - QPS).
- Storage requirements for rich media (PDFs, Images, Audio).
- Ownership of Meta WhatsApp API conversation charges (whether Meta per-conversation fees will be borne directly by PTA or included in the vendor’s bid).

### 2. Contradiction in Delivery Timelines (27 Days vs 2 Years Contract)
In Section V (Required Services, Page 31), the **Delivery Schedule** is explicitly stated as **27 Days**, whereas Section Scope of Work (Page 33 & 34) states that services are to be hired for **Two Years (24 Months)**. This creates legal and contractual contradiction regarding liquidated damage enforcement (GC Clause 20) and project milestones.

### 3. Inappropriate Selection Method (Least Cost Based Selection - LCBS)
The procurement adopts **Least Cost Based Selection (LCBS)** under Rule 29/30. Awarding hosting of a critical national telecom authority chatbot solely to the lowest bidder without a mandatory minimum technical qualification threshold (Quality and Cost Based Selection - QCBS) exposes PTA to severe cyber security vulnerabilities, data breach risks, and frequent downtime from unverified low-cost hosters.

### 4. Blank and Unpopulated Price Schedule Formats
Page 36 and Page 69 of the SBD contain unpopulated price schedule templates without clear line-item divisions for Cloud Hosting, Bandwidth, Meta API fees, and 24/7 Managed Support SLA.

## SECTION 2: TECHNICAL DEFICIENCIES & AMBIGUITIES

1. **Meta WhatsApp Business API Cost Allocation**:
   Meta charges per 24-hour conversation session. In a public-facing national chatbot, message volume can reach millions. If Meta API charges are forced onto the vendor without a capped usage model or direct PTA billing agreement, no vendor can provide a fixed PKR price for 24 months.
2. **Backend API System Readiness & Integration Protocols**:
   The ToRs mandate integration with PTA CRMs, ERPs, DIRBS, and ticketing systems, but do not specify whether REST/SOAP APIs, OpenAPI specs, staging environments, or VPN tunnels are currently available or yet to be developed.
3. **Data Residency & Cyber Security Compliance**:
   The SBD does not clarify whether hosting must strictly reside within Pakistan (e.g. NTC / Local Sovereign Cloud) or if global cloud infrastructure (AWS / GCP / Azure) is permitted under Pakistan Cyber Security Regulations.
